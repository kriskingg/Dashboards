## Shared-ingress boundary

The following two URLs are different applications:

```text
NIFTY options paper research
https://triumph-events-chair-problems.trycloudflare.com/live_latest.html

MCX / Silver hedge research
https://triumph-events-chair-problems.trycloudflare.com/mcx_latest.html
```

They share ingress and the served report directory, but they do not share generator ownership.

```text
live_latest.html -> generated by NIFTY multi-shadow code in etf-invest-engine
mcx_latest.html  -> generated by hedge-engine
```

Do not infer code ownership from the common hostname or port 8765.
